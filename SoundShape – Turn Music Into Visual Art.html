    About SoundShape

    A free, private audio visualizer built on the Web Audio API and HTML5 Canvas.

    Reacts to real audio

    An analyser splits your track into bass, mid and treble bands that drive every bar, ring, wave and particle.

    Private by design

    Files are played from local memory and never leave your device. Library entries disappear on refresh; your settings are saved.

    Copyright-safe

    No commercial music is included. Use tracks you own or have rights to. The demo beat is generated on the fly.
